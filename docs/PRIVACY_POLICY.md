## Summary

- You can use every part of the app **without an account** and without giving
  any personal details.
- Personal details are collected **only if you choose to send the donation
  details form** ("Contact Now"), and only to confirm your offering and send a
  receipt.
- The app shows **no advertisements**, does **not track you** across apps or
  websites, and we **never sell or share** your information for marketing.

## Information we collect

### 1. Donation details you choose to send
When you use *Donate → Contact Now*, we receive what you type: your **name**,
**mobile number**, and optionally your **e-mail**, **amount**, **purpose**,
**payment method**, **transaction / UTR number** and **message**. The app also
adds the **app language, platform (Android/iOS) and app version**.

*Why:* to match your offering with the temple's records, contact you about it
and send a receipt. *Legal basis:* your consent, given when you press *Submit*.

Your name, number and e-mail are also **remembered on your phone** so you do not
have to type them again. You can remove them at any time with *Clear saved
details* under the form. They never leave your phone except inside a form you
submit.

### 2. Notification token
If you allow notifications, the app receives a **Firebase Cloud Messaging token**
for your device and subscribes it to one topic matching your app language. This
is used only to send utsav, katha and temple announcements. Turn it off in
*Settings → Utsav notifications* or in your phone's settings.

### 3. Crash reports
If the app crashes or hits an error, **Firebase Crashlytics** may send a
technical report: device model, operating-system version, app version, an
installation identifier, and the technical details of the error. Reports do
**not** contain your name, phone number, e-mail or donation details. Turn this
off in *Settings → Share crash reports*.

### 4. Content you open
Kirtans, photos, shastra PDFs and other content are downloaded from Google
Firebase. Like any web request, this involves your device's IP address, which
Google processes to deliver the content. Files you download for offline use stay
on your phone.

### 5. Maps
The *Prasadi Places* map, Street View and directions use **Google Maps**. When
you use them, Google may receive your device's IP address and the map area you
view. Google Maps is subject to the
[Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service](https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
The app does **not** request or use your location.

### Permissions the app asks for
- **Notifications** — for utsav and katha announcements (optional).
- **Photos (add only)** — only when you choose to save a photo or the donation
  QR code to your phone.
- **Motion sensors (iPhone)** — only inside the 360° darshan, to look around by
  moving your phone.

## How the information is stored and shared

- Information is stored in **Google Firebase** (Google Cloud), in the
  **asia-south1 (Mumbai)** region, and encrypted in transit.
- Donation details are visible only to authorised temple office staff. If the
  temple has turned on e-mail alerts, a copy of each request is e-mailed to the
  temple office address.
- Google acts as our service provider (processor). We do not share your
  information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- **Donation details:** deleted from the app's database within
  **[12] months** after the receipt is issued. Records the trust must keep by
  law (for example for accounting or Section 80G receipts) are kept in the
  trust's own books, not in the app.
- **Notification token:** until you turn notifications off or uninstall the app.
- **Crash reports:** kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
